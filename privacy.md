# Loop Sync privacy policy

Loop Sync is a personal habit tracker based on Loop Habit Tracker.

- Your habits and check-ins are stored on your device.
- If you turn on sync, a copy is saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Loop Sync can only access that folder.
- Your data is not sent anywhere else. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.
- You can turn sync off in the app's settings, or remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/connections
